# Legal readiness — qualified review required

Engineering review dated 2026-10-05. This is not legal advice, legal certification or a final contract. Public processing of real mail is blocked pending qualified review and truthful owner-specific notices.

Israeli Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 took effect on 2025-08-14. The product must identify the actual controller, processing purposes, lawful collection/authorization, recipients, rights handling and cross-border arrangements. Counsel must assess database registration/notification and DPO applicability based on actual activity and scale; an email service may receive specially sensitive information even when metadata is the primary input. Counsel must review the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations and any relevant EEA-origin information requirements. Filing a notification is not regulatory approval.

Source: [Privacy Protection Authority Amendment 13 information](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy-protection-regulations/privacy-protection-regulations/), [official guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/guide_tikon13_professional/he/tikun%2013%20_170825.pdf), [database notification service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/notice-obligation), [security regulations translation](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/data_security_regulation/en/PROTECTION%20OF%20PRIVACY%20REGULATIONS.pdf). The English regulations translation itself disclaims legal status; counsel must work from authoritative Hebrew law.

Draft terms must cover: identified provider/contact; user authority to connect the mailbox; explicit cleanup authorization; Gmail trash and provider-controlled permanence; protections and known undo limits; service dependencies/outages; beta limitations; acceptable use; account termination/export/deletion; optional AI processing consent; governing law and consumer rights; liability clauses reviewed for enforceability. Do not call aggregate size reductions actual recovered Google storage: moving mail to Trash is not immediate permanent removal.

Also review Israeli accessibility applicability/statement, customer communications and direct-marketing rules if any marketing is added, subprocessors/cross-border transfers, incident response duties and user rights. Do not introduce auto-unsubscribe sending without authorization and technical verification.

FAIL launch gates: controller/contact fields incomplete; contracts/notices unsigned; processor regions/agreements unverified; public privacy/terms/accessibility pages unapproved; deletion/retention and backup behavior not end-to-end tested; verification/assessment evidence missing. Fix by completing the legal facts and implementing controls, then have qualified counsel and the release manager review the resulting artifacts. No automated test can waive these items.
