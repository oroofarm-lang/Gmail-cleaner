# Accessibility statement

Draft status: private engineering preview, 2026-10-05. Not an approved public notice or legal certification. Provider legal name, registered address, privacy/support contact and applicable terms require owner completion and qualified review before public real-mail launch. Owner fields have not been invented.

Inbox Agent targets WCAG 2.2 AA. The UI includes semantic headings/navigation, keyboard focus styles, a skip link, labelled inputs, live status/error regions and native modal dialogs. It respects reduced motion and supports light/dark appearance. Automated checks and source review alone do not demonstrate full conformance.

Known review gaps include screen-reader coverage of every view, final contrast/reflow checks, mobile navigation focus behavior, dialog focus return and exact review of live cleanup results. Public accessibility conformance and Israeli legal applicability require independent testing and qualified review. No certification is claimed.

An accessibility contact and alternative support/accommodation process have not been supplied. They must be published and tested before public use. Users must be able to report a barrier with affected view/browser/assistive technology without sharing mail content. The owner must record response procedures and update this statement with actual audit date, limitations and completed fixes.
