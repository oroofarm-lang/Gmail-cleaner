## Mailbox changes and recovery

Inbox Agent exposes moving messages to Gmail Trash, archiving and restoring confirmed app-owned actions; it does not expose permanent deletion. Gmail controls Trash expiry and recovery. Gmail normally removes Trash after 30 days; you may permanently delete items directly in Gmail sooner. The app's current undo path rejects actions older than 29 days and may fail if the provider no longer has the message. Never rely on Inbox Agent as a mailbox backup. Trash movement is not proof of immediately recovered account storage.

Provider errors can leave a mutation uncertain. Check Activity and Gmail before retrying. Undo covers successful app-owned actions, not edits by other clients. Demo cleanup/unsubscribe/undo affects synthetic data only. Live unsubscribe requires a manual sender/Gmail action; the app does not automatically contact arbitrary unsubscribe targets.
