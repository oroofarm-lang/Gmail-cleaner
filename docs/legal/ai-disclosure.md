# AI disclosure

Draft status: private engineering preview, 2026-10-05. Not an approved public notice or legal certification. Provider legal name, registered address, privacy/support contact and applicable terms require owner completion and qualified review before public real-mail launch. Owner fields have not been invented.

Privacy Mode and the demo assistant use deterministic command matching and rule compilation. In Smart Mode, when configured, user-command intent interpretation calls OpenAI; an explicit Analyze metadata button can send limited sender/subject/labels for advisory classification. A configured provider key does not establish successful live verification. Synthetic demo replies are labelled deterministic; they do not prove real model processing.

The repository includes a separate optional OpenAI Responses adapter that accepts bounded metadata, requests strict structured output with `store:false`, validates it again and has no action tools. Activating it requires accurate informed Smart Mode consent, provider/model configuration and reviewed processing/retention agreements. Privacy Mode must send no mail metadata to an AI provider. Sender/subject/snippet/labels may still contain sensitive personal information; excluding bodies does not anonymize them. Provider retention depends on actual account/contract settings; `store:false` does not mean zero retention.

AI advice can be inaccurate or manipulated by malicious email content. It must remain a proposal. Users approve supported consequential actions, and deterministic protections may reject a model's suggestion. No AI output may authorize permanent deletion, arbitrary network fetches, credential access or cross-tenant operations. Customer mail is not used to train product models by this application. Any broader provider-training/retention statement requires verified provider terms and configuration.

Autonomous maintenance, live guardian monitoring and extension AI pairing are unavailable. Public claims must match actually exercised features. Any future activation or new provider must update this disclosure and privacy notice before processing.
