# Responsible disclosure

Draft status: private engineering preview, 2026-10-05. Not an approved public notice or legal certification. Provider legal name, registered address, privacy/support contact and applicable terms require owner completion and qualified review before public real-mail launch. Owner fields have not been invented.

The owner has not supplied a security reporting email or supported secure reporting channel. This document is not an active disclosure program. Establish and test that channel before public launch.

When an authorized channel exists, report the affected version/URL, minimum reproducible steps, expected/actual result and security impact. Use synthetic or your own disposable data. Do not include OAuth tokens, encryption keys or other users' email contents. Avoid unnecessary access, modification, persistence or service disruption. Stop once impact is demonstrated and notify the owner privately through the verified channel.

Acknowledgement and remediation timelines must be chosen by the owner and supported by monitoring; none is promised here. Safe-harbor wording, authorized test scope and bounty eligibility require owner/counsel approval. No bounty or permission to test third-party infrastructure is implied. Mailbox providers and hosting platforms maintain their own reporting programs.
